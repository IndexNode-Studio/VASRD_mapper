🛑 Mandatory Pre-Qualification Gatekeepers (Must Satisfy Before Adjudication Pathway)

Before the VA will evaluate your claim (or if you are already service-connected and seeking an increase), confirm these 3 Eligibility Gatekeepers are documented in your medical file.

1. Chronicity & Continuity of Symptomatology (38 CFR § 3.303(b)) Inspect Acceptable vs. Disqualifying Evidence ▾
✅ What Qualifies (Acceptable): Continuous medical chart records, specialist notes, prescription history, or objective test/imaging results spanning from discharge (or prior rating) to today.
❌ What Disqualifies (Fails the Gate): An isolated complaint in service with zero subsequent medical follow-up or treatment history.
2. Continuity of Care & Treatment Compliance Inspect Acceptable vs. Disqualifying Evidence ▾
✅ What Qualifies (Acceptable): Regular consultations with doctors, specialists, taking prescribed medications, or using medical devices (CPAP, orthotics, braces).
❌ What Disqualifies (Fails the Gate): Total lack of treatment records or refusal to seek ongoing medical care.
3. Competent Lay Observation (Jandreau v. Nicholson / Symptom Logs) Inspect Acceptable vs. Disqualifying Evidence ▾
✅ What Qualifies (Acceptable): Detailed symptom logs, pain diaries, and VA Form 21-10210 buddy/spouse statements describing daily functional impacts and flare-ups.
❌ What Disqualifies (Fails the Gate): Unsupported assertions without formal medical diagnosis or buddy corroboration.
