How to Use This VASRD Reference Tool

This guide explains how to navigate, search, and use the checklists and reference materials on this site.

1. Searching & Browsing Conditions

  1. Search by Keyword or Diagnostic Code: Use the search bar on the main page to filter conditions by name, body system, or 4-digit VASRD diagnostic code.
  2. Review Rating Criteria: Open any condition to view the specific percentage tiers (0% to 100%) and the exact symptoms or clinical findings required for each rating level under 38 CFR Part 4.

2. Using Pre-Qualification Checklists

  • Evidentiary Alignment: Check off symptoms, clinical measurements (such as Range of Motion), and medical history items that match your current medical records.
  • Identify Gaps: Use unchecked items to see what medical documentation or C&P exam measurements are typically needed before filing a claim or increase.

3. Saving & Printing Your Notes

  • Local Browser Storage: Any notes or checklist selections stay locally in your browser session and are not uploaded to an external server.
  • Print or Save as PDF: Use the built-in Print/Export button (or press Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) to save a clean PDF copy of your condition summary for personal records or to bring to a VSO appointment.
